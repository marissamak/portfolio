About Me

A bit about me

Hi, I'm Marissa. For four years I've designed brands, websites, and campaign collateral for freelancers, small businesses, and a national not-for-profit. I work best with clients who know what they want and value clear communication from brief to delivery.

I build custom sites in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, or work in WordPress and the usual page builders when that suits the project. Based in Brisbane, open to remote work worldwide. Past clients across Australia, Hong Kong, Taiwan, the United Kingdom, and Japan. Have a rebrand, site, or campaign in mind? Send a brief and tell me what you're working on.
