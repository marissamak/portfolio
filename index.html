About Me

A bit about me

Hi! I'm Marissa. I've been designing for seven years, mostly branding, marketing bits and pieces, and websites for clients who want to look polished without it feeling stiff.

I started in uni saying yes to every creative brief I could find. That turned into freelancing, and I also worked in-house at a not-for-profit on marketing and comms: campaigns, sites, reports, slide decks, the lot.

For websites, I can code from scratch (HTML, CSS, JavaScript) when you want something fully custom, or work in WordPress, Squarespace, Wix, Webflow, Shopify, Elementor, and the usual page builders. Landing pages, portfolio sites, refreshes, booking flows, and GitHub Pages deploys are all in the mix.

I like clients who know roughly what they want and are happy to chat things through. I'm in Brisbane right now and moving to Sydney soon, open to local and remote work. Planning a rebrand, a new site, or one piece of design? Send a brief or a quick hello. I'd love to hear what you're working on.
