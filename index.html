About Me

Thoughtful design, any brief

Hi! I'm Marissa — a graphic designer with five years of experience creating brand identities, marketing collateral, and digital design that helps clients show up with confidence.

I got my start in uni saying yes to every creative project I could, and that hobby eventually grew into a proper freelance career. I have experience working full-time in marketing and comms at a not-for-profit, where part of my job includes designing everything from fundraising campaigns to websites, reports, and decks.

Whether it's a full brand refresh, a one-off campaign, or a website build, I love collaborating with clients who care about strong visuals and clear communication. I'm open to projects across industries — from hospitality and events to corporate, creative, and not-for-profit work.
